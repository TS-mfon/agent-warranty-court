Autonomous adjudication · Base · GenLayer

When one agent pays another,
someone has to judge the work.

Agentic commerce standardised the happy path — identity, negotiation, payment. Nothing handles the moment an agent hallucinates a citation, breaks its output schema, or silently fails while still charging.

This is the missing layer. Validators independently fetch the provider's published SLA and signed execution transcript, check the claims against live web ground truth, and reach consensus on breach. Escrow settles on Base. No human in the release path.

The core problem

A contract cannot read an SLA. Validators can.

⛔ What Solidity cannot do

  • Read "responses must cite a verifiable source" and decide whether a response did
  • Fetch a cited URL and notice it returns 404
  • Judge whether prose answered the question it was paid for
  • Tell a schema violation from a stylistic choice

✓ What this protocol adds

  • Validators fetch the SLA and the transcript and re-derive the verdict independently
  • Ground truth is probed live — a dead citation is observed, not asserted
  • Evidence is hash-committed before adjudication, so it cannot be swapped mid-trial
  • The verdict is a closed enum, so an injected instruction cannot invent a payout

Why it is safe to use optimistically

The relayer can delay settlement. It cannot steal it.

A verdict reaches Base through a bonded relayer, which is the only party in the system that is merely bonded rather than trusted. Every invariant Solidity can check on its own is checked on its own, regardless of what any verdict claims.

01

Recipients are a closed set

A payout can only reach the job's client or its provider. No verdict field names an address, so a fully successful prompt injection still cannot introduce one.

02

Payouts are bucketed

Five values, nothing between. Not a simplification — independent validators converge reliably on a five-value set and never on a free integer.

03

The ceiling is per case

A payout can never exceed what that job escrowed, so one verdict cannot reach another job's money or a provider's free stake.

04

Terminal means terminal

Once a job has paid out, no later verdict, cure window or settlement can move it again — which is also what makes double-settlement unreachable.

Read this before trusting it

The trustless backstop is not active

The design calls for a challenged verdict to be settled by proving GenLayer's finalized state on Ethereum and forwarding it to Base. Neither half could be deployed, so a challenge instead resolves by timeout after 14 days, with the posted verdict standing and the challenger forfeiting its bond. The relayer is bonded, but not provably falsifiable.

GenLayer demonstrably settles to Ethereum Sepolia — 383,307 batches committed, verified and executed — but neither inclusion-proof selector is registered on its chain diamond, and on its Bridgehub a real selector and a deliberately nonexistent one revert identically. The interface cannot be established from outside, and a forwarder wired to a guessed verifier would look live while verifying nothing.
